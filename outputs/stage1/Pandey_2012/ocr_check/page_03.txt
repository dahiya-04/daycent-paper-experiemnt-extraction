2.2. Raising of crops

Experiments were conducted during 2009 and 2010 in the same
well drained field as used in previous years on the same rice culti-
var (PHB-71). During both the years of experiment, sowing of rice in
RNT-WCT and RNT-WNT was done on 20th June. On the same day,
seeds were soaked for the nursery raising of rice to be transplanted
in RCT-WNT and RCT-WCT. Seedlings were transplanted in puddled
bed on 16th and 19th July in 2009 and 2010, respectively. Recom-
mended doses of nutrients (150 kg N+ 75 kg P205 + 75 kg K2O) were
applied through 15:15:15 NPK complex fertilizer and urea. Full
doses of P and K and 50% of N was applied through 15:15:15 NPK
complex at the time of sowing or transplanting. Rest of 75 kg N was
applied as urea in two equal splits at critical tillering and panical ini-
tiation stages. Herbicides were used as and when required. In no till
drill sown rice (RNT-WCT and RNT-WNT), pre emergence spray of
pendimethalin 30 EC was applied at the rate of 1 kg ha~!. However,
in hand transplanted rice (RCT-WCT and RCT-WNT), pre emergence
application of Butachlor 50 EC was done at 1.25 kg ha~'. Herbicide
application was accompanied with one hand weeding at 25 days
after sowing/transplanting. This practice was followed every year
since start of tillage experiment. After attaining the maturity, the
crops were harvested on 30th October in 2009 and 3rd November
in 2010.

2.3. Measurements of fluxes of CH4, N20 and CO2

Fluxes of CO2, CH4 and N20 from soil were measured during
rice crop period during 2009 and 2010 following manual closed
chamber technique (Hutchinson and Livingston, 1993). The Perspex
chambers with volume of 0.05019 m? (30cm x 23.9cm x 70cm)
had separable bases made of Perspex. The bases of chamber sys-
tems were installed 24h before the deployment of chambers in
order to bring the system into equilibrium and eliminate the effect
of soil disturbance on fluxes. Chambers were fixed firmly on water
filled rim of respective bases. A battery operated fan and a pump
were used to circulate air at 1.51 min~! to ensure uniform mixing of
air inside the chamber. Water levels inside the chambers were mea-
sured during flooded conditions. Temperature inside the chamber
was recorded by the thermometer attached. Effective volume inside
the chamber was corrected for plants present inside by measuring
average above ground biomass volume of three plants harvested
weekly. The chamber had one sampling port on the top fitted with
non reactive rubber septum. Samples were drawn for CH4 and
N20 with 20 ml air tight syringes at 0, 20, 40, 60, 80, and 120 min
and transferred immediately into pre-evacuated vacuum tubes of
4ml capacity (Vacutainer, Becton, Dickinson & Company, NJ, USA)
and analysed on gas chromatograph (CP 3800, Varian, CA, USA)
the same day. Concentration of CH, in the gas samples was esti-
mated with flame ionization detector (FID) while electron capture
detector (ECD) was used for estimating N20. Gases were separated
on same Stainless steel column packed with PORAPAK-Q (50-80
