found to be gradual from 0 to 150 min. After 150 min, the infiltration rates
slowed down gradually and attained a pseudo-steady state after 210 min,
irrespective of the tillage and crop-residue treatments. The infiltration rate
after 300 min ranged from 2.0 mm/hr under Tirpwo to 4.0 mm/hr under
TotW. It is thus apparent that the basic infiltration rate was slightly higher
