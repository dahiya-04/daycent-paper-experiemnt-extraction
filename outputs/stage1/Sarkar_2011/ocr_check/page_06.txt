Soil Analyses

The experimental soil was analyzed for basic chemical and physical prop-
erties following standard methods as follows: pH in water was measured
by glass electrode and cation exchange capacity following the neutral
normal ammonium acetate extraction procedure (Jackson 1967; Hesse 1994;
Table 1).

Soil Organic Carbon

The impacts of puddling, tillage, and crop-residue incorporation, along with
rate of inorganic N application for rice-wheat system in changing soil pro-
ductivity, were evaluated by assessing temporal organic carbon content of
soil. Soil samples were collected from 0-15, 150-30, and 30-45 cm depth
from each treatment combination at the end of three years of the experi-
ment (October 2003), processed, and kept in a dry place for further analysis.
Organic carbon content of soil was estimated by the modified Walkley-Black
method (Walkley & Black 1934).
