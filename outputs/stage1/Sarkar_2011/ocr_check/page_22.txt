As was discussed in earlier sections, SHC and IR were lower under inten-
sive puddling than less intensive puddling, which increased the moisture
retention in soil, maintained suitable conditions for TR growth, and ulti-
mately resulted in more yield (Saroch & Thakur 1991). Incorporation of
crop residues in 2002 and 2003 increased the yield of TR highly signif-
icantly over when no residues were added. The yield of TR reached its
maximum when both rice and wheat residues were added. Application of
crop residues improved the soil structure, contributed to increased SOM
content, improved nutrient-holding capacity of soil, and maintained yield
potential (Bauer & Black 1981; Bhandari et al. 2002; Hutsch, Augustin, &
Merback 2002). Similarly, in the case of DR, yield was significantly higher
