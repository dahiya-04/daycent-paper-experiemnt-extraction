Residue Treatments

rate. The rate of increase in SOC with increasing N application rate was
found to be higher with the application of crop residue. It reached its maxi-
mum when both rice and wheat crop residues were applied (Figure 4). The
linear increases in SOC with N application rate under all residue treatments
revealed that the generation of biomass increased with increasing level of
