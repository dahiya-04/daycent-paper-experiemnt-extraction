Particulate Organic Carbon (POC)

Particulate organic C was analyzed taking after the strategies
depicted by Sollins et al. [18]. Briefly, 20 g soil was defloc-
culated by adding 100 ml of sodium hexametaphosphate. The
soil solution was shaken for 18 h with 90 rpm min~’ in a
reciprocating shaker. The suspension was washed with 53 um
screen with the consistent stream of distilled water. The rest of
the soil on the screen was washed, collected, and dried (60 °C
for 12 h) for estimation of POC.
