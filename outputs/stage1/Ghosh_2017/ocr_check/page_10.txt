Conclusion

The findings thus conclude that long-term inclusion of
chickpea (alternate-year) in RW and MW rotations can
improve POC, labile C-fractions (Cfrac, and Cfracz),
and TOC. The improvement in soil properties with
inclusion of chickpea was clearly reflected in base-crop
productivity. The scale of improvement in soil C in
chickpea inclusive rotation was larger when crop residue
was returned to the soil. Addition of crop residue and
FYM was found to have important role in enhancing soil
